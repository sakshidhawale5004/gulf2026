Select Your Jurisdiction

Mainland

Set up a mainland company in UAE through the Department of Economic Development (DED) and gain unrestricted access to the local UAE market, prime business locations, and government contracts. Mainland companies allow 100% foreign ownership in most sectors and can trade freely with other businesses across all emirates.
Learn more

Freezone

UAE free zones offer 100% foreign ownership, 0% corporate tax on qualifying income, and the freedom to operate internationally. With 40+ free zones available — including DMCC, IFZA, and RAKEZ — foreign investors can find a jurisdiction that matches their industry, budget, and business activity.
Learn more

Offshore

Register an offshore company in UAE through designated jurisdictions such as RAK ICC or JAFZA to benefit from 0% tax, full confidentiality, and no requirement for a physical office. Offshore structures are ideal for international trading, asset protection, and holding companies.
Learn more

Why Choose Dubai For Starting A Business?

Dubai is a strategic global business destination offering world-class infrastructure, a strategic location connecting major markets, tax-friendly policies, political stability, and a diverse, innovative economy. Its vibrant environment, ease of business setup, and excellent growth opportunities make Dubai highly attractive for entrepreneurs and investors globally.

What Support Does Dubai Offer Entrepreneurs Starting A Business?

Dubai offers extensive support to entrepreneurs through simplified business setup processes, competitive licensing options, investor visas, and dedicated free zones with numerous incentives, including tax exemptions. Moreover, networking opportunities and government-backed initiatives help businesses grow from inception to expansion.

What Are My Options For Company Formation In The UAE, And How Much Does It Cost?

You can establish your business in a Freezone, Mainland, or Offshore jurisdiction, each offering unique benefits. Freezones provide 100% foreign ownership and tax advantages, Mainland companies offer access to the local UAE market, and Offshore entities are ideal for international business without a physical presence.

The cost of setting up a business in Dubai depends on the chosen jurisdiction, business activity, and license type. A&A Associate provides transparent pricing and tailored solutions to help you select the best structure aligned with your business goals.

What Kind Of Business Licenses Are Available In Dubai?

Dubai offers various business licenses, including Commercial, Professional, Industrial, and Tourism licenses. Each license type caters to specific business activities, such as trading, consultancy, manufacturing, or hospitality. A&A Associate helps businesses choose the right license based on their industry and operational needs.

How Long Does The Company Formation Process Take In Dubai, And What Documents Are Required?

Company formation typically takes anywhere from three weeks to several months, depending on the business type, jurisdiction, and required legal procedures. The necessary documents usually include passport copies of shareholders, visa copies (if applicable), proof of address, a business plan or company profile, and specific industry approvals based on the chosen activity. A&A Associate provides a detailed checklist to ensure a smooth setup process.
