General

GulfTP is a transfer pricing benchmarking database built specifically for the GCC region, giving multinational enterprises and tax professionals access to over 100,000 comparable companies for their compliance work.

GulfTP is built for multinational enterprises, tax professionals, and transfer pricing advisors operating in or serving the GCC region.

Coverage

GulfTP covers six GCC and regional markets: UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Egypt, and Oman.

Databases

GulfTP offers four specialised databases: the Gulf Company Database, Interest Rates Database, Services Database, and IP Licensing Database.

It provides comparable company data for general transfer pricing benchmarking across the GCC region.

It provides reliable data for benchmarking intercompany loans and financing arrangements across the Gulf.

It supports benchmarking of intra-group service transactions.

It supports benchmarking of royalty arrangements and intellectual property licensing agreements.

Process

It follows a four-step process: (1) Select your database, (2) Apply filters such as industry, company size, and location, (3) Analyze the comparable data, then (4) Export a report for your documentation.

You can book a search directly through the Book Search option, request a custom GCC-specific benchmark, or schedule a one-on-one consultation with a specialist.

Access & Plans
